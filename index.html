How to add ticket types
First, General automatically uses your full Event Ticket Quantity. To add another type such as VIP, first reduce the General quantity by the number of tickets you want to give to VIP. Then click + Add Ticket Type and enter the VIP quantity.
Example: Event Ticket Quantity 15 → General 10 → add VIP 5 → total 15/15. Ticket types can never exceed the event total.
